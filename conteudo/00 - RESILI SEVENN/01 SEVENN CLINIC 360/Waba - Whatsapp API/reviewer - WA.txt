[1] Which app functionality requires this permission
Our application is a multi-tenant CRM that allows business clients to connect
their own WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) via Meta's Embedded Signup flow.
The whatsapp_business_messaging permission is required to:
- Send and receive messages on behalf of the connected client's WABA
- Display the client's WhatsApp inbox inside our CRM dashboard
- Trigger pre-approved message templates (HSM) for transactional and
  marketing flows configured by the client

Without this permission, clients cannot use the WhatsApp channel inside
our CRM, which is the core feature of our product.

[2] How the permission enhances the integration
This permission powers the entire WhatsApp module of the CRM:
- Two-way inbox: client agents reply to customer messages from a unified UI
- Template campaigns: clients send approved HSM templates to opted-in contacts
- Automation flows: bots qualify leads before routing to human agents
- Contact sync: every conversation is linked to a CRM contact/opportunity

[3] How the permission improves user experience
- End business users (our clients) manage all customer conversations in
  one place instead of switching between WhatsApp Web and the CRM
- Their customers receive faster, more contextual responses because the
  agent sees full CRM history alongside the conversation
- Lead qualification is automated, freeing agents for high-value chats
- Templates are tracked and reported on the same dashboard as other
  marketing channels
